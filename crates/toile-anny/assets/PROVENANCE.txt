Toile — crates/toile-anny/assets/body.bin — provenance
=======================================================

This asset is baked by `toile anny-bake` (crates/toile-cli/src/anny_bake.rs)
from 378 files under naver/anny's src/anny/data/mpfb2/, and from nothing
else: the neutral template (base.obj, basemesh_vertex_groups.json) plus 376
`.target.gz` morph targets — the adult tier described below. No NAVER code
is included, run, or shipped: the bake reads plain-text mesh data, JSON and
gzip, and the station-assignment rule, the quad-to-triangle split, the
phenotype math and the asset format are all reimplemented in Rust from
scratch.

What the bake keeps
--------------------
Only the `body` vertex group (13,380 of base.obj's 19,158 vertices, and its
13,378 quad faces): the neutral hm08 base mesh's skin, not the helper
geometry, joint cubes, hair, teeth or genital helpers the same file carries.
The 125 `joint-*` vertex groups from basemesh_vertex_groups.json are read
only for their centroids, to assign each kept vertex a Station tag; none of
their vertices are themselves shipped in the asset.

Every kept target's line whose vertex index falls below 13,380 becomes one
delta entry, quantized to an `i16` per axis as thousandths of a decimetre
(the source text carries at most three decimal places, so this quantization
is exact — the baker asserts it file by file). A weighted target's row also
carries the 26-bit phenotype mask its filename mapped to; a `measure-*`
target instead carries a lever id and an incr/decr direction. See
crates/toile-anny/src/phenotype/ for the mask and lever vocabulary, and
crates/toile-anny/src/asset/ for the row and delta byte layout.
